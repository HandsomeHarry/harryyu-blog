## What it is

The 944 predates touchscreens by four decades, so the dash had no place for navigation. This is a 3D-printed ABS housing for the center dash that fits a motorcycle CarPlay unit like it belonged there.

## How it's built

Modeled in Fusion 360 against measurements of the original dash opening, printed in ABS for heat tolerance, finished and fitted to the car. See the [garage log](/garage/carplay-housing) for the install.
